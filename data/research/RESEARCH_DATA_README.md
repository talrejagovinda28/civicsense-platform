# Authority research dataset — NOT approved production seed

This folder preserves the 2026-09-22 research workbook and CSV exports from its five research sheets. Every record must be evaluated using its own source, date, caveats and verification status. The filename `RESEARCH_ONLY` is intentional: do not auto-activate an external channel, assign every city issue to PMC, mark all officer assignments current, or imply the 165 corporators are fully verified. Historical ward-office labels may be stale.

CSV rows are byte-level exports of workbook cell values, not a new external verification pass. Imported official names are informational data until verified against up-to-date official records and linked to the correct jurisdiction/service asset and accepted channel. Unavailable current data remains unknown; do not generate placeholders in production.

See `docs/05_AUTHORITY_DIRECTORY_AND_ROUTING.md`, `docs/13_RESEARCH_SOURCES_AND_GAPS.md` and original workbook caveats before using in seeding code.
